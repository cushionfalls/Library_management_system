═══ LIBRARY POLICIES ═══
• Wallet: Users can top up their wallet via Stripe (minimum $0.01, maximum $1,000). All amounts are stored in cents internally but displayed in USD. The wallet is used to rent or buy books and pay fines.
• Book Rentals: Users can rent physical books (in-hand) or rent/buy online digital copies. Rental periods and pricing are set per book by the librarian.
• Fines: Overdue books incur fines. The fine amount is deducted from the user's wallet. Users can check their balance on the Wallet page.
• OTP Verification: New accounts require email OTP verification before login. OTPs expire after 5 minutes.
• Membership Plans: Three tiers — Basic (30 days, $5.00), Standard (90 days, $12.00), Premium (365 days, $35.00). Active membership unlocks certain online books at no extra cost.
• Book Reviews: Users who own or have membership access to a book can leave a rating (1-5) and written review. One review per user per book.
• Account: Users can update their profile, change password, and upload a profile picture from the Profile page.
